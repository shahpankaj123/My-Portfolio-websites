LinkedIn post Client platform, code is private

CampusDesk is the Catholic Board of Education's school operations platform. More than 50 schools work through one connected system instead of separate tools, and each area of school life is owned by its own service.

What it covers

  • Identity and authentication with OAuth2
  • School administration, student and staff workflows
  • Fee management and reporting
  • Transport, exams, attendance and homework
  • Staff leave and library operations

Architecture

  • Independent Spring Boot services on Java 21, built with Maven
  • Database per service: each service owns its schema, so teams can change one domain without touching another
  • Service discovery with Eureka and service-to-service calls with OpenFeign
  • Event-driven communication over Apache Kafka keeps services in sync without shared tables
  • PostgreSQL for persistence, with Redis alongside
